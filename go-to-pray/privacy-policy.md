# Go To Pray — Privacy Policy

English · [Français](privacy-policy-fr) · [العربية](privacy-policy-ar)

**Effective date:** October 24, 2026

Go To Pray is a free prayer-times app for LG webOS TVs, made by AbdulBasset Chouman. This policy explains what information the app uses and where it goes. In short: **the app does not collect, sell or share personal information, and it has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.**

## What stays on your TV

The app stores the following on your TV only. It is never sent to the developer or anyone else.

- Your settings: the country, state/province and city you chose; the prayer-time and Hijri calendar calculation methods; your notification choices for each prayer; and your language (or, if you haven't chosen one, your TV's language).
- Today's prayer times and Hijri date, kept so the app works quickly and can remind you even when it isn't open.
- A short technical log used for troubleshooting. It stays on the TV and is not sent anywhere.

Uninstalling the app removes all of this from your TV.

## What is sent over the internet

To calculate prayer times and the Hijri date, the app's background service contacts the **AlAdhan API** (api.aladhan.com) about once a day and whenever you change your location or calculation method. Each request contains only:

- the city and country you chose,
- the calculation methods you chose, and
- the date being calculated.

It does not include your name, your account, your TV's identifiers or anything about what you watch. As with any internet request, AlAdhan's servers can see your internet (IP) address; their handling of it is covered by AlAdhan's own terms at <https://aladhan.com/credits-and-terms>.

The app makes no other internet connections.

## Notifications

Prayer reminders are shown with your TV's built-in notification system. When you choose "Alert", the app opens on screen at prayer time and, if you turned it on, plays the adhan. All of this happens on the TV.

## Your TV and LG

Go To Pray runs on LG's webOS platform. Information that LG or your TV collects on its own is governed by LG's privacy policy, not this one.

## Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Open data

The list of countries, states and cities shipped with the app comes from the Countries States Cities Database by dr5hn (ODbL 1.0). Our adapted copy is published at <https://github.com/abedshouman/lg-st-apps-data/tree/main/go-to-pray>.

## Changes to this policy

If the app's handling of information changes, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change.
