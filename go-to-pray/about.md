# Credits & licences

**Go To Pray** is a free prayer-times app for LG webOS TVs, made by AbdulBasset Chouman. It has no ads, no in-app purchases and no tracking — see the [privacy policy](privacy-policy).

## Prayer times and Hijri dates

Provided by the [AlAdhan API](https://aladhan.com). AlAdhan's calculations are based on the methods and formulas of [Pray Times](https://praytimes.org).

Prayer times are calculated and may differ slightly from your local mosque. They are provided without any warranty, as described in [AlAdhan's terms](https://aladhan.com/credits-and-terms).

## Adhan recording

*Masgid Sultan Hassan: Sheikh Said: Adhan Recitation* — Sheikh Said Refi'ai at the Sultan Hassan Mosque, Sayeda Aisha, Cairo, recorded 1 February 2010 by Anna Kipervaser / On Look Films, LLC, for *Voices and Faces of the Adhan: Cairo* (World Oral Literature Project). Published by the [University of Cambridge repository (Apollo)](https://www.repository.cam.ac.uk/items/73daed49-989e-40c0-92be-9b2602c25532) under [CC BY-NC-ND 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-nd/3.0/). Included in the app unmodified.
